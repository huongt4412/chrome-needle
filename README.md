# 🧠 chrome-needle - Control your browser with plain English, fully offline

---

## 🎉 What Is This?

chrome-needle is a free Chrome extension that lets you control your browser by typing or speaking normal sentences. Instead of clicking through menus and typing commands, you can tell chrome-needle what you want—like "fill this form with my name and email" or "go to the next tab"—and it does it for you.

The magic happens because chrome-needle contains a small but powerful artificial intelligence (AI) model called Needle 2. This AI runs right inside your browser using a technology called WebAssembly (WASM). No internet connection is needed. It works 100% offline, so your private information never leaves your computer.

chrome-needle can help you:
- Control browser tabs (open, close, switch, search)
- Fill out forms automatically with your saved data
- Interact with web pages (click buttons, read text, navigate)
- Run custom scripts you write yourself (if you want to get fancy)

### Step 2: Install the Extension

Once you have downloaded the file, you need to load it into Chrome. Here's how:

1. Open your Chrome browser.
2. Type `chrome://extensions` in the address bar and press Enter.
3. In the top-right corner, turn on **Developer mode** (switch it to blue/on).
4. You will see new buttons appear at the top-left: **Load unpacked** and **Pack extension**.
5. Click **Load unpacked**.
6. A window will open asking you to select a folder. Navigate to where you extracted or saved the chrome-needle files. Select that folder and click **Select Folder**.

That's it! chrome-needle will now appear in your extensions list with a small icon in your browser toolbar.

## 🛠️ How to Use chrome-needle

chrome-needle is designed to be simple. Here are some examples of what you can say or type:

### Tab Control 🗂️
- "Open a new tab"
- "Go to my Gmail"
- "Close this tab"
- "Switch to the next tab"

### Form Filling 📝
- "Fill my name in this form"
- "Autofill my email address here"
- "Submit this form"

### Page Actions 🌐
- "Click the login button"
- "Read the main text on this page"
- "Scroll to the bottom"

### Custom Scripts 🤖
- "Run script: highlight all links"
- "Script: refresh this page every 5 minutes"

To use it:
1. Click the chrome-needle icon in your browser toolbar (top-right, near the menu dots).
2. A side panel will open on the right side of your screen.
3. Type your command in the text box, or use the microphone icon to speak it.
4. Press Enter or click Send.

chrome-needle will process your request and perform the action. It will show you what it did in the side panel.

## ⚙️ System Requirements

For chrome-needle to run smoothly, your computer should have:

- **Operating System:** Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
- **Browser:** Google Chrome version 109 or newer
- **Memory (RAM):** At least 4 GB (8 GB recommended)
- **Processor:** Any modern CPU (Intel Core i3 or equivalent, from 2015 or later)
- **Storage:** About 200 MB of free space for the extension files

chrome-needle uses your computer's CPU to run the AI model. It works fine on most modern laptops and desktops. If your computer is older, it might take a few seconds longer to respond.
